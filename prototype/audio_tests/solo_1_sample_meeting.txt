# Solo audio test 1: the sample meeting, read by ONE person (you play both people)
#
# How to read it
#   - Read the lines in order, in your normal voice. Skip the label (dis/dec/act)
#     and the name: start at the words after the colon.
#   - Between lines: stop for a full 2 seconds (count "one-thousand, two-thousand"
#     in your head). The checker hears a pause of more than 0.8 s as the other
#     person starting to talk, so this is how one voice plays two people.
#   - Inside a line: keep going, no stop longer than half a second, even at a
#     full stop or comma. A long breath mid-line splits it into two turns.
#   - Any recorder is fine (phone voice memo, laptop). Quiet room, phone about
#     30 cm away. Save it as .m4a, .wav or .mp3.
#
# Then run (needs: pip install openai-whisper, and ffmpeg):
#   python prototype/audio_check.py prototype/audio_tests/solo_1_sample_meeting.txt my_solo_1.m4a
